## Cleared — do not re-derive

Checked against an independent derivation, not just read.

| quantity | verdict |
|---|---|
| the joint V-space model `V = A[1 − S + K·masses]·B`, `S = Σmasses = λ` | correct and identifiable. `K(0, r) = 1` for every r, so `V(0) = A` exactly and `S` is the modulation depth — `A` and `S` are separately identifiable and there is no redundant scale parameter, as the docstring claims |
| the width floor `σ_res = r⁴/(27·ν_dd·T)` | the **3** is exact: `f(r) = ν_dd/r³` ⇒ `|df/dr| = 3ν_dd/r⁴`, and `δr = δf/|df/dr|` with `δf = 1/T` gives `r⁴/(3·ν_dd·T)`. Units: nm⁴/(MHz·nm³ · µs) = nm, since MHz·µs = 1. The remaining **1/9** is an asserted fit-efficiency factor, not a derivation — the code comment says so. Ablated below |
| `aic` / `aicc` / `bic` | standard Gaussian-likelihood forms; `kpar = 3N + n_bg + 1` counts the Gaussian parameters, the leading background parameters, and the noise variance — the correct count |
| component `area = a·σ·√(2π)`, `weight = area/Σarea` | correct |
| linearized covariance `pinv(JᵀJ)·RSS/(npts − nparams)` | standard; `pinv` is the right guard for the near-singular directions overlapping components produce |
| support-plane threshold `SSR ≤ SSR_min·(1 + F_{1,N−q}(level)/(N−q))` | matches Hustedt 2015 / the standard support-plane construction |
| the stale-floor guard in `_solve` | works: components migrating to long r end up sitting on **their own** re-applied floor (measured at 0.0539 / 0.0865 / 0.1476 nm for centres at 2.95 / 4.37 / 4.99 nm), not on the seed centre's |
| `_gauss_seed_centers` | correct (local maxima ranked by height, padded with an even spread) |

`_pake_transform` is a trapezoidal cosine transform with halved end columns —
correct — but it carries a factor **2** (the one-sided convention) that the
docstring's stated formula `∫F(t)cos(2πνt)dt` does not. Harmless, because the
same factor multiplies both `Fnu` and `Kfreq` and cancels in the MSD. *note.*

## The two design questions the plan asked — both answered by ablation

Every row is a paired comparison against `base.json` on the same 156 runs (same
cases, conditions, seeds). Patched copies of `deer.py` live in
`~/deer_benchmark/s5_gauss/mods/`, generated by `mkvar.py`; `cmp.py` does the
pairing.

### 1. Multi-start seeding: it is the *strategy*, not the count

The plan asked whether the round-8 improvement "comes from the seeding *count*
rather than the strategy. Test against a random-restart control." It does not.

| variant | Δ overlap | t | correct-N | bit-identical |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **base** — Tikhonov-peak seed + even-spread seed | — | — | **0.801** | — |
| `seed1` — Tikhonov peaks only | −0.0081 | −3.31 | 0.718 | 62 % |
| `seedrand` — 2 seeds, the second one random | −0.0033 | −1.89 | 0.776 | 58 % |
| `seed4rand` — **4 purely random** restarts, ~4× the cost | −0.0064 | −2.25 | 0.731 | 0 % |

Four random restarts — double the seed count and four times the compute — recover
about a fifth of what the single designed spread seed buys, and every one of their
results differs from the shipped one (0 % bit-identical), so this is a genuinely
different sampler that simply loses. The two designed seeds are complementary: the
Tikhonov-peak seed carries information a random start throws away, and the
mass-support spread is the de-clustering escape. The losses under `seed1`
concentrate on the 3- and 4-component truths, which is the mechanism the docstring
at `deer.py:2716-2724` describes.

### 2. The width floor `r⁴/(27·ν_dd·T)`: the 1/9 is not a refinement, it is the rescue

| variant | Δ overlap | t | correct-N | k-collapses | bit-identical |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **base** — constant 27 | — | — | 0.801 | 8/104 | — |
| `floor_c3` — the **derived** Rayleigh limit `r⁴/(3·ν_dd·T)` | **−0.1449** | −12.15 | **0.295** | **31/104** | 2 % |
| `floor_c9` | −0.0206 | −4.59 | 0.673 | 14/104 | 10 % |
| `floor_c81` | −0.0096 | −4.29 | 0.808 | 8/104 | 31 % |
| `floor_off` — flat `s_lo`, no distance-dependent floor | −0.0104 | −4.32 | 0.808 | 8/104 | 30 % |

The derivation is right and the *derived quantity is the wrong floor*. Imposing
`δr = r⁴/(3·ν_dd·T)` — exactly the resolution limit the comment derives — costs
**0.145 overlap** and drops correct-N from 0.80 to 0.30, because it forces every
long-r component to be at least that broad. What ships is that limit divided by 9,
and the code comment presents the 9 as "a ~1/9 fit-efficiency factor". The
measurement says the 9 is not a refinement of the derivation; it is what stops the
derivation from destroying the fit.

The floor is nonetheless worth having: turning it off costs **−0.0104 overlap
(t = −4.32)**. But 27 sits on a narrow ledge — one step tighter (9) costs
−0.0206, one step looser (81) is indistinguishable from having no floor at all
(−0.0096 vs −0.0104, and 31 % vs 30 % of runs bit-identical to base). And it buys
its overlap at a small cost in N-accuracy: correct-N is **0.801 shipped against
0.808 with the floor off or inert**.

*note.* The docstring and `deer.md` should say the 27 is calibrated, not derived —
they currently present it as a physical resolution limit with a small correction.

### 3. `prune_spurious` is a coin flip on this catalogue

| variant | Δ overlap | t | correct-N | runs changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `noprune` | −0.0007 | −0.75 | 0.795 vs 0.801 | 4 % |

Pruning fires on **6 of 156 runs** and its outcome is mixed:

| run | criterion N → pruned N | true N | overlap |
|---|---|---|---|
| easy `g1_broad` σ0.005 | 2 → 1 | 1 | 0.972 → **0.995** |
| easy `g3_close` σ0.005 | 4 → 3 | 3 | 0.954 → **0.974** |
| hard `g2_uneven` σ0.005 | 3 → 2 | 2 | 0.908 → **0.949** |
| nobg `g4_uneven` σ0.02 | 4 → 3 | 4 | 0.720 → **0.831** |
| easy `g4_uneven` σ0.02 | 4 → 2 | **4** | 0.825 → **0.793** |
| nobg `g3_even` σ0.04 | 4 → 2 | 3 | 0.706 → **0.649** |

On two of its six firings it deletes components the ground truth has — in
`easy/g4_uneven` the criterion had picked the *correct* N = 4 and pruning cut it to
2. Net effect over the catalogue is +0.0007 overlap at t = 0.75, i.e. nothing, and
correct-N is 0.6 points *worse* without it, also nothing.

This is the pattern the roadmap's *Known tensions* section warns about: pruning was
justified in round 8 against a baseline that lacked the joint V-space fit, the
multi-start and the width floor, all of which landed under it. The docstring already
concedes it is "now just a light backstop" (`deer.py:2494`); the measurement says
the backstop catches almost nothing and misfires a third of the time it does fire.
It is documented in `deer.md` with a full tip box and two tunable constants.
**If this stack is ever simplified, `prune_spurious` is a candidate to drop
alongside `echo_head`** — but not on this evidence alone: the catalogue is
exact-N Gaussian truths, which is the regime pruning was *not* built for.

### S5-1 · `deer_validate(engine='gauss')` produces a band that cannot mean anything · **bug**

`deer_analysis.py:2125` · `deer.py:3368`

The Multi-Gaussian tab's **Validate** checkbox is structurally inert. The `'lsq'`
solver's least-squares target is `V_tgt = bg['V_norm']`, and both
`joint_background` (`deer.py:1354`) and `background_fit` (`:184`) set
`V_norm = V/_echo_top(t, V)` — **no `bg_start` anywhere in it** — while `A` and
`k` are free leading parameters that re-fit the background from scratch. One
skeptic measured `max|ΔV_norm| = 0.000e+00` across `bg_start ∈ {0.3, 0.6, 1.2,
2.0}` µs: the residual function is *bit-identical*, so a background-start sweep
can only move the optimiser's starting point.

The ensemble that comes out is flat-valley jitter, rendered as a percentile band:
**`P_spread/P_scale` = 8.7e-05 for gauss/joint against 5.2e-02 for Tikhonov** on
the same trace and sweep — a factor 600 — after ~27 s (10–11× a plain fit).
Independently found by the GUI reviewer (`validate-1`) and the call-sites
reviewer (`callsites-7`, which put the gauss band ~1000× narrower than the
Tikhonov engines' on the identical sweep).

**Both skeptics CONFIRMED the core and broke three of my sub-claims:**

* *"9 identical curves / `P_lower == P_upper`"* — **false as a literal.** Trials
  differ by 1.9e-6 to 8.4e-3 of peak, and one real trace (`sample3_labA` under
  `sequential`) spans 0.020 nm in `r_mean` with N flipping 4→3→4. An equality
  test would never have fired.
* *"`disagree` is False by construction"* — **REFUTED.** The per-trial flags come
  from the prep dict, which *is* rebuilt at every `bg_start`. Measured
  `disagree = True` with 6/9 flagged. **The flag half is live and the fix had to

  *"validation trials disagree (mean spans 0.00 nm, 6/9 flagged)"* — a
  self-contradiction — and *"trial spread: mean 0.000 nm"*, which reads as
  *background-insensitive, i.e. robust*.
* my `bg_engine='none'` caveat was wrong: inside the ±7.5 % grid `deer_validate`
  actually uses, `'none'` is degenerate too (1.2e-4 nm). My 0.144 nm came from a
  0.65 µs move far outside the grid.

**Fixed** in the shape the second skeptic's fix-gate argued for, not mine.
`deer_validate` returns `trial_spread['P_spread']`, `['P_scale']` and
`['band_degenerate']`; the gauss condition is **structural, not a threshold** —
`engine == 'gauss'` with any `bg_engine` but `'general'`, exactly the set where
the background is co-fit — because the skeptic showed the numeric gap (gauss to
8.4e-3 vs Tikhonov 5.2e-2…1.3e-1) is only ~6×, too thin for a silent GUI
behaviour. The 1 %-of-scale threshold survives only as a secondary catch for
other engines. The GUI draws **no band**, says why, and the status reads
`(9-trial sweep, no band)`. The flag half is untouched.

### S5-4 · the `'mc'` band measures optimizer spread · **bug, and my headline was confounded**

Confirmed 2/2. Three corrections, the first of which retracts my framing:

* **"the reported uncertainty ANTI-correlates with the noise" must be struck.**
  My demo compared σ 0.005 (N=3) with σ 0.02 (N=2): it is an **N flip, not a

* measured coverage is **0.27–0.72 (mean ≈ 0.41) against a nominal 0.95**, and
  0.00 whenever the ensemble collapses. The S4-5 precedent shipped `ci_kind`

The wrong claim also lives in `deer.py:2320-2321` / `:2545-2551` and
`deer.md:171` / `:418-420`, which **recommend this band as coverage-honest**.

## Other measurements taken this session

### `'mc'` versus `'lsq'`: the docstring's claim is false

`deer.py:2547` says *"On artifact-free synthetic data 'mc' ties 'lsq'"*. Over the
104 `easy` + `hard` runs of the known-N catalogue, artifact-free by construction:

| | overlap | correct-N | k-collapses | wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `lsq` | 0.8754 | 0.808 | 8/104 | — |
| `mc` | **0.8451** | **0.644** | **17/104** | 41 % |

Δ = **−0.0302 overlap, t = −5.46**. Not a tie. And on the 28 **real** traces the
two solvers of the *same engine* disagree by a mean of **0.419 nm** in peak
position, worst case **3.789 nm** — while `mc` costs 76 s per trace against 48 s.

A likely mechanism, from the reviewer: the `'mc'` path's σ lower bound is the flat
`s_lo` (`deer.py:2361`), **not** the distance-dependent `_sigma_floor` the `'lsq'`
path uses — so the mode advertised as the one that "cannot be trapped in the
floor-width-spike solution the gradient fit can fall into" is the one without the
spike guard. Unverified.

### Linearized CI coverage: honest for one mode, blind to mode-swaps

40 noise realizations per truth, N forced to the true value, interval read as
`centre ± 1.96·center_err`:

| truth | centre coverage | reported sd / true scatter |
|---|---|---|
| one Gaussian at 3.0 nm | 1.00 | 1.13 |
| one Gaussian at 4.5 nm | 0.95 | 0.96 |
| two Gaussians, 2.8 + 4.4 nm | 0.95 | **0.02** |

For a single well-determined mode the bar is well calibrated — that is worth
recording as a *pass*. On the two-component fit the reported sd is **50× smaller
than the across-realization scatter** while coverage still reads nominal, because
the scatter is carried by a minority of realizations that land on a qualitatively
different solution. The local-quadratic bar cannot see a mode swap, and coverage
alone would not have revealed it. `S5U-6` adds the bound case: a component pinned
at the σ floor is reported as `σ = 0.0539 ± 0.0872`, an interval **61 % of whose
length lies outside the feasible region**, including negative widths.
